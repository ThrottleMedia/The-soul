04 / things we wonder at 2:17 a.m.

The beautiful
unanswerables.

Not every mystery needs solving. Some questions simply make the world feel bigger.

QUESTION 001

The observer

Does a moment become more real when someone remembers it — or does memory quietly remake the moment?

QUESTION 002

The ship

If every part of you changes with time, what makes you still you? Your body, your memories, or the story you tell?

QUESTION 003

The coincidence

Are some meetings fate, or are humans meaning-makers who turn chance into a constellation?

QUESTION 004

The now

We spend today remembering yesterday and preparing for tomorrow. When do we actually arrive?

These are philosophical thought experiments, not claims of secret proof. Wonder is welcome; certainty can wait.
